Australia's Native Mammals

Diversity, distribution and conservation in Australia

Australia is home to a remarkable variety of native mammals, from marsupials and monotremes to bats and rodents. Many occupy specialised habitats, and some face serious conservation challenges. This visualisation compares nationally listed mammals to explore how conservation status varies between mammal groups and across Australia.
